Supacom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Supacom (“the app”) is a companion client for Supabase. We take a minimal-data approach.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Your Supabase access token

To function, the app requires a Supabase access token that you provide. This token is stored only on your device, in the iOS Keychain. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. It is sent solely to Supabase’s own API to access the projects and data in your own Supabase account, at your direction.

Your data

All project and database content shown in the app lives in your own Supabase account. We never receive, store, or have access to it.

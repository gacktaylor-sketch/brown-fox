Supacom — Support

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Supacom is a companion app for Supabase. It lets you check project health and usage, browse and edit table rows, view auth users, and read logs from your phone.

Getting started

Connecting your Supabase account

Supacom needs a Supabase access token to reach your projects. Create one from your Supabase account page at supabase.com/dashboard/account/tokens, then paste it into Supacom when prompted. The token is stored only on your device, in the iOS Keychain.

Removing your token

Sign out from within the app to erase the stored token from your device. You can also revoke the token itself at any time from the Supabase dashboard page linked above, which immediately cuts off access.

Common questions

The app says my token is invalid

Access tokens can be revoked or expire. Generate a fresh one from the Supabase dashboard and sign in again. Make sure you copied the entire token with no extra spaces.

I don't see all of my projects

Supacom shows the projects your access token can reach. If a project belongs to an organization you're not a member of, it won't appear. Check your organization membership in the Supabase dashboard.

My data isn't loading or looks out of date

Pull to refresh on any screen. If it persists, confirm the project is running and healthy in the Supabase dashboard — Supacom reflects whatever Supabase reports.

Is Supacom affiliated with Supabase?

No. Supacom is an independent third-party client. It is not made, endorsed, or supported by Supabase. It talks to Supabase's public API using credentials you provide.

Reporting a bug or requesting a feature

Email gacktaylor@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and app version (shown in Settings), plus what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Please don't include your access token in any message.

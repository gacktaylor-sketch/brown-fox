Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

This policy explains how Squatrax (“the App,” “we,” “us”) handles information. The App measures squat depth from videos you film or choose. It has no servers of its own, and it does not collect any information about you.

The short version: There is no account and no sign-up. Your videos, measurements, and history are processed and stored on your device and are never sent to us. The App shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking.

1. Information we do not collect

  • No account, login, or sign-up is required or offered.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, videos, photos, or health information.
  • We operate no servers and no backend. There is nowhere for us to send your information even if we wanted it.
  • The App contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

2. Camera, microphone, and your videos

The App uses the camera to film your set, and the microphone to record the audio that goes with that video. You can also choose an existing video to analyse. Body positions are detected from the video using Apple’s on-device frameworks, and the depth of every rep is calculated on your device. Your videos and the measurements taken from them are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

3. Information stored on your device

Your sets — the videos, measurements, weights, targets, and history — your settings, and your purchase entitlement are stored locally in the App’s own storage. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and is deleted when you delete a set or delete the App. It may be included in your own device backups under your Apple account settings.

4. Sharing and saving

If you choose to save a judged clip to Photos, or to share one through the iOS share sheet, it goes only where you send it. Saving to Photos uses add-only access; the App does not read your photo library. What happens to a clip after you share it is governed by the service you shared it to.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The App receives only a confirmation that a purchase completed, and records on your device that you own it. See the Apple Privacy Policy for how Apple handles that transaction.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and it is not offered in a children’s category. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). If you have a concern, email us and we will help.

7. Security and retention

Because your data lives only on your device, its security depends on your device’s own protections. We hold no profile about you, so there is nothing on our side to retain, breach, or hand over.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, a request to access or delete your data is satisfied on your device: delete a set from the Journal to remove it, or delete the App to remove everything.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date above reflects the current version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@brownfoxgo.com.

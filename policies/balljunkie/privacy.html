Ball Junkie — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Ball Junkie mobile application ("the App") handles information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, or contacts. In the current version, the App does not collect or transmit personal data about you.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your progress in the in-app puzzle game is saved locally on your device only. It is not uploaded to us and is removed if you delete the App.

Data Requests

The App retrieves football statistics and reference data from our hosted read-only database. These are standard network requests. As with any internet service, our hosting provider may automatically log technical information such as IP address and request time for security and operational purposes. We do not use this information to identify you.

Advertising (Future Versions)

A future version of the App may display third-party advertisements through Google AdMob. When advertising is enabled, AdMob and its partners may collect and process information such as device identifiers, ad interaction data, approximate (non-precise) location, and usage information in order to serve and measure ads. On iOS, if any tracking across apps or websites is involved, you will be asked for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you may decline.

When this feature ships, the App's App Store privacy details will be updated to reflect the data collected. You can learn how Google uses this information in the Google Privacy & Terms and manage ad personalization in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your Choices

You can limit ad tracking at any time through your device's privacy settings (Settings → Privacy & Security on iOS), and you can remove all locally stored data by deleting the App.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.

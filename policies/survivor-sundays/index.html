Survivor Sundays

Support · Brown Fox LLC

Pick one team a week. Win and advance, lose and you're out, and no team twice all season. Survivor Sundays keeps the picks, the standings and the trash talk in one place so the commissioner doesn't have to.

Need help?

Email contact@brownfoxgo.com and we'll get back to you. If it's about a specific league, include the league name. If it's about a purchase, include the email address on the account.

Common questions

How do I join a league? Ask whoever runs it for the league key, then enter it on the join screen. You can also scan the invite QR code.

Can I change my pick? Yes, any time before that game kicks off. Once it kicks off, it's locked.

I bought premium and don't see it. Premium is tied to your account, not your device — make sure you're signed in with the same email you paid with. If it still isn't there, email us.

How do I delete my account? Email contact@brownfoxgo.com from the address on the account and we'll remove it along with your picks and posts.

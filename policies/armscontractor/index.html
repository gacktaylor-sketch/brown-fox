There is no account and no server — so there is no password to reset and nothing to log in to.

I bought Prime Vendor Status and it is not showing

Open the Prime Vendor icon on the desktop and tap Restore a previous purchase. The unlock is tied to the store account that bought it, so use the same Apple ID or Google account. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of purchase.

What does Prime Vendor Status do?

It is a one-time purchase. It removes advertising, transfers capital into your current company once, and lets you choose your opening capital every time you register a new company.

How do I get rid of the ads?

Prime Vendor Status removes the full-screen ad that can appear when you advance the week, permanently. The two rewarded ads stay available, but only ever play when you choose to watch one.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

Open Prime Vendor and tap Ad privacy options. That control appears in regions where it is required. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings — iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads.

I keep losing money on contracts I win

Every number that decides a deal is on screen before you commit: the supplier price, the freight, any rush fee, the margin, the due-by date and the arrival date. If the arrival is after the due-by, you are late — late deliveries dock 25% a week down to a 10% floor. Check the arrival date before you confirm the fill, and use the Spread app to compare a line item across every house in your book.

Why can I not bid on some notices?

Greyed notices tell you what you are missing — usually a supplier who carries that commodity. Contacts arrive by referral, one for each order you place, so the way to open up the board is to keep doing business. What size of work appears at all depends on your standing.

I lost my company

Your company is stored only on your device. It is not backed up to us, because we have no servers. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes it permanently and we cannot recover it. Prime Vendor Status can be restored; the company cannot.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything the game stores — your company, its ledger, and the record of whether you own Prime Vendor Status — lives on the device and goes with it. Nothing is held anywhere else.

Is any of it real?

The setting is: 2004, real agencies, real procurement terminology, real equipment designations, all matters of public record. Everything you actually deal with — the companies, contacts, solicitations and prices — is invented for the game. No weapon is shown in use and nothing is aimed at anyone. None of it is procurement or export-control advice.

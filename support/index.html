One inbox for every Brown Fox app. Write to us and a person will read it.

Email us

contact@brownfoxgo.com

We answer as quickly as we can — usually within a couple of days.

What to include

It helps us sort things out on the first reply:

  • Which app you're writing about, and your device and iOS version.
  • For a purchase problem — the email address on the account you bought with, and roughly when you bought it. Never send card numbers.
  • For a bug — what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. A screenshot is worth a paragraph.
  • For an account or data request — write from the address on the account so we can verify it's you.

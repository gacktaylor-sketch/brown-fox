What the app is

Survivor Sundays keeps track of survivor-style football pools. You join or create a league, pick one team each week, and the app records who advanced and who is out. It is a record-keeping and scoreboard tool for a group that already knows each other.

No money changes hands in this app. Survivor Sundays does not collect entry fees, hold a pot, set prizes, or pay anyone out, and it is not a gambling or wagering service.

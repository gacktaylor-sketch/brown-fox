Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026

This policy covers brownfoxgo.com and the internal tools Brown Fox LLC operates for its own business. Each app we publish has its own privacy policy describing what that app does — those are the ones that matter if you are a player, and they are listed under App Policies.

The short version: this website has no accounts, no logins, no forms, no analytics and no cookies of our own. It is a set of static pages. We do not collect anything from you here.

1. This website

  • No account, sign-up, or login exists on this site.
  • There are no contact forms, comment fields, or trackers, and we set no cookies.
  • We run no analytics of our own on these pages.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (including IP addresses) for security and abuse prevention. Those logs are GitHub's, not ours — see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

2. Our apps

Every app we publish carries its own privacy policy, because what each one collects differs. Find yours under App Policies. In general our apps have no accounts and no servers of ours: saved progress lives on the device, and the only third party involved is the ad network where an app shows ads. Two exceptions are stated in their own policies — apps that use a backend for accounts or payments say so plainly.

3. Internal tools

We operate private, single-user tools that read our own business data — App Store Connect sales reports and AdMob earnings for apps we publish — through the providers' APIs, using credentials belonging to Brown Fox LLC. These tools run locally, are not offered to anyone else, and never receive or request data belonging to any other developer or any end user.

4. Data we hold about you

None, from this website. We keep no mailing list and no customer database. If you email us, we have your email and whatever you wrote in it, kept only as long as it takes to answer you.

5. Children

This site is not directed to children and collects nothing from anyone, including children. Age suitability for each app is stated on its App Store listing and in its own policy.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data from this site, there is nothing here to access, correct, port, or delete. For data held by a specific app, see that app's policy. For anything else, email us and we will do what the law requires and what is reasonable besides.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as the business changes. The effective date above reflects the current version.
